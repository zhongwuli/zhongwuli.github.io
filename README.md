## About

I am a Postdoctoral Scientist at the Ellison Institute of Technology Oxford (EIT Oxford) and a Visiting Researcher in the Department of Chemistry at the University of Oxford.

My research focuses on molecular transport under nanoscale confinement and its translation into biomimetic nanochannels, soft ionic devices, membrane separations, energy conversion, and intelligent biointerfaces.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL) and an Affiliate Visiting Researcher at the Molecular Foundry, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL).

## Website Maintenance

This website is built with Jekyll and the al-folio academic website template.

To update the website:

1. Edit the relevant files in `_pages/` or `_data/`.
2. Commit changes to the `main` branch.
3. GitHub Actions automatically builds and deploys the website.
4. Check the Actions tab to confirm successful deployment.

For detailed instructions, see [WEBSITE_EDITING_GUIDE.md](WEBSITE_EDITING_GUIDE.md).
