I am **Zhongwu Li**, a **Postdoctoral Scientist** in **Materials & Devices for Life Sciences** at the **Ellison Institute of Technology Oxford (EIT Oxford)**. I am also a **Visiting Researcher** in the **Department of Chemistry, University of Oxford**.

Before joining EIT Oxford, I was a **Postdoctoral Researcher** in the **Materials Science Division at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL)**. During this appointment, I was also an **Affiliate Visiting Researcher** at the **Molecular Foundry, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL)**.

Previously, I was a **Lecturer** at the **China University of Mining and Technology**. I received my doctorate in **Mechanical Engineering from Southeast University**, including joint doctoral research training at LLNL. 

My research focuses on fluid transport phenomena under nanoscale confinement and their translation into advanced membrane separations, energy conversion systems, and neuromorphic functional materials. 

## Research Interests

**Nanofluidics.** Development of biomimetic nanochannels, combined with experimental and computational studies of fluid transport at nanoscales, elucidating interface, confinement, and quantum effects.

**Bioiontronics.** Development of 3D droplet interface bilayer networks, engineered protein nanopores, and hydrogel- and ion-based iontronic memristors for bioinspired information processing and biointerfaces.

**Water, Energy & AI.** Translation of nanofluidic and bioiontronic principles into energy conversion, molecular separation, intelligent sensing, and neuromorphic ionic computing.

## Research Vision

My long-term aim is to bridge **fundamental nanofluidic physics** and **functional bioiontronic devices**, connecting molecular transport phenomena with engineering technologies for water, energy, and AI.
