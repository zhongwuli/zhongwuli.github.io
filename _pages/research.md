Since my doctoral training, I have investigated nanoscale fluid transport through molecular dynamics simulations and continuum modeling, elucidating the fundamental mechanisms governing transport at solid–liquid interfaces and under extreme confinement. At LLNL, I expanded my research to experimental investigations of molecular-scale transport, probing water, ion, and proton transport through biological and synthetic nanochannels using fluorescence spectroscopy and electrophysiology. More recently, I have extended this work into neuromorphic ionic computing and biointerfaces through the development of iontronic memristors. Collectively, my research bridges fundamental nanofluidics and bioiontronics with practical engineering applications, addressing challenges in water, energy, and AI.
